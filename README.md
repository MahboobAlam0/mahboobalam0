## Selected Work

<table>
<tr>
<td width="50%" valign="top">

### Distill
<sub>Text-to-SQL Assistant</sub>

Fine-tuned Qwen2.5-3B with QLoRA on 28K Text-to-SQL pairs, turning a model that almost never produced the exact query into one that gets nearly a third right. Served with FastAPI and Docker.

**2.33% &rarr; 30.67%** exact match

<sub>`PyTorch` `QLoRA` `PEFT` `FastAPI` `Docker`</sub>

</td>
<td width="50%" valign="top">

### AI Clinical RAG
<sub>Clinical Question Answering</sub>

A retrieval-augmented pipeline that grounds answers in clinical sources, with semantic search on Qdrant. Most of the gain came from getting the right passage to the top.

**70.0% &rarr; 82.3%** Accuracy@1

<sub>`Python` `Qdrant` `Sentence Transformers` `FastAPI`</sub>

</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td width="50%" valign="top">

### PIAUNet
<sub>Underwater Image Segmentation</sub>

A physics-informed Attention U-Net for segmentation in aquaculture, where underwater imaging conditions make standard models struggle. Published research.

**97.38%** mIoU

<sub>`PyTorch` `Computer Vision` `Segmentation`</sub>

</td>
<td width="50%" valign="top">

### HMT-ECGNet
<sub>Lightweight ECG Classification</sub>

A compact CNN for ECG classification that keeps strong accuracy at a fraction of the usual size, deployed as an API.

**0.92** Macro-AUROC &nbsp;·&nbsp; **0.34M** params

<sub>`PyTorch` `CNN` `FastAPI`</sub>
